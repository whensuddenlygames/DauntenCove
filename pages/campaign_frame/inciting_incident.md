Many countries denounced those who claimed themselves to be Living Gods. None more so than the self-proclaimed greatest nation, the Unified Empire. The fraying diplomatic relationship between the U.E. and their rivals the Grand Isles only worsened when the military queen of the Grand Isles formally recognized the divinity of the Living Gods.

Fearing the Living Gods would usurp the worship of the Dreaming Gods of old, the U.E. and its allies banded together and drew up articles called the Edict of Heresy. Within the document, they denounced the “Heretical Gods.” They demanded on nations sign the edict that formally called for the destruction of these “demons who have come to destroy our most ancient traditions and ways of life.” Yet only a handful agreed to sign the edict: The Unified Empire, Svaardust, and Levnika (Viscaria and the Syndicum Arcanus joined at later dates).

The rest refused to be strong armed by the Unified Empire, claiming their edict to be driven not by piety but by politics. Several viewed the move as an attempt to demonstrate their global might.

In the year [XXXX], a group of Unified sailors made landfall in a small port town of Esmeralda within the country of Bernadette. Reports are unclear as to what happened next. As close as it has ever come to be understood, a fight erupted between at local bar between the sailors and locals over the validity of the edict and the new, or heretical, gods. In the ensuing brawl Admiral Pravlov Izbek of the Unified Empire was slain, igniting the powder keg of war.

The remaining soldiers returned to their ship and bombarded the small town with heavy artillery in retaliation. Bernadettan militias were not ready for such an attack. As best as they were able, they mustered a small resistance but it was no match for the overwhelming force of the Unified Empire. Esmeralda was seized by the sailors, refusing to leave until proper recompense could be paid.

The Bernadetten parlament refused.

The War of Reformation had begun.
