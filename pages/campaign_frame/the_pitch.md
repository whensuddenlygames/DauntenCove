*Read this to your players*

> The world war ended at the port city of Daunten Cove. Not because of a stalemate. Not because cooler heads had prevailed. No, the War of Reformation came to a disquieting halt when a Living God was torn to ruin by the entity known as Entropy.
> 
> A tenuous cease-fire agreement has been met between the armies of the Reformation and the Orthodoxy. A greater threat seeks to destroy the fabric of reality. Soldiers who were once enemies must now push past their ideological differences, take up their arms, and fight for the world itself.
>
> Check the landing gear, load the tank shells, and dive deep inside your submarines. You are soldiers for the world, you are soldiers of Daunten Cove.

# Tone And Feel

Post-World War Tension, Epic, Heroic, Diesel Punk, Expansive, Eldritch Horror, Fantasy

# Themes

Authoritarianism, Religious War, Divine Right, Demonology, People vs. gods, Exploration, World War

# Touchstones

The Great Escape, Dirty Dozen, Band of Brothers, Enemy at the Gates
