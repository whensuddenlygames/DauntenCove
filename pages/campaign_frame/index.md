This is just a slice of the setting that has been in some form of development for over 6 years. To keep in the spirit of a Daggerheart Campaign Frame, I have purposefully left out a lot of setting specific names, maps, and NPCs. What you'll find within this frame is enough information to get you started and able to expand on the world and story your table wishes to tell. 

If you don’t care for anything else but the new piloting rules, then head over to [Pilot Domain] and have a blast. Though this is just a small portion of what I have planned for this ruleset.

I hope you enjoy *Daunten Cove*.
