# Vehicle Piloting Rules

**Base Concept:** Anyone can pilot any vehicle. When taking over the pilot seat, replace 1 of your Domain cards with the Pilot Domain card. This card allows you to perform Basic Piloting Maneuvers.

## Ranges
Vehicle Ranges remove the Melee Range entry. For naval and aircraft, you can multiply the ranges by 10 for a more accurate gauge on distances.

## Basic Piloting Maneuvers
Player must succeed on an Agility Reason Roll (13) or mark a Stress to perform one of three basic maneuvers. 

+ Move Left
+ Move Right
+ U-Turn

Maintaining forward movement always succeeds. 

## Maneuverability

Each Vehicle will have its own item card. On that card will be the vehicle's maneuverability rating which represents how many Ranges it moves when performing any maneuver. For instance, a single seat fighter plane may have a maneuverability of 1. Meaning it only moves a single range increment when performing a maneuver. A massive gunboat may have a maneuverability of 3, causing it to move 3 ranges whenever it performs a maneuver. 

## Pilot Domain
On level up players can pick up advanced Pilot Domains. These offer abilities such as auto-passing basic maneuvers. More to come.

## Vehicle Cards
Vehicles will have unique item cards. Each card lists the vehicles potential weapons, maneuver rating, stress rating, HP, armor, and any special abilities or rules.

### Stress
When performing any maneuver which requires the player to mark a Stress, it is taken from the vehicle's Stress.

## Environments
When piloting a vehicle, the player also enters into a basic Tier 1 Event Environment appropriate to the vehicle. The Tier can be modified based on the narrative, such as facing off against a more seasoned adversary pilot.
